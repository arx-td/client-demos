Questions

Frequently asked

Do I have to starve or do a crash diet?
Never. The whole program is built around real, everyday food — the opposite of crash dieting. You'll eat enough to feel good while still losing weight.
Is it safe with PCOS, diabetes or fatty liver?
Yes — the plan is designed specifically with these conditions in mind. That said, it complements (not replaces) advice from your own doctor, and we always take your health history into account.
Is the coaching online?
Yes. Everything is done online, so you can join from anywhere in the UK, Pakistan or beyond — with regular check-ins and support.
How much weight will I lose?
Most people lose around 5–10kg in 45 days. Results vary from person to person depending on your body, condition and how closely you follow the plan.
